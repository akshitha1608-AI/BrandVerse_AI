FAQs

Built for transparent collaboration

How are match scores calculated?

Scores use a weighted, rule-based model: category 30%, budget 20%, audience 20%, content format 15%, objective/skills 10%, and availability 5%. The breakdown and gaps are returned for each match.

Are the profiles and metrics real?

No. Seeded accounts and profile metrics are clearly labeled as fictional local demonstration data.

Do recommendations use a paid AI API?

No. Recommendations and campaign brief drafts are generated locally with deterministic rules.
